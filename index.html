Geometry Formulas

• Area of circle: A = πr2

• Circumference of circle: C = 2πr

• Area of rectangle: A = ℓw

• Area of triangle: A = 12bh

Volume Formulas

• Rectangular prism: V = ℓwh

• Right cylinder: V = πr2h

• Sphere: V = 43πr3

• Right cone: V = 13πr2h

• Pyramid: V = 13Bh

Right Triangles & Angles

• Pythagorean Theorem: a2 + b2 = c2

• Special Right Triangles:

- 45°–45°–90°: x, x, x√2

Constants & Facts

• Radians of arc in a circle = 2π
