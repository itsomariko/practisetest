College Board SAT Math Reference Sheet

Geometry Formulas

• Area of circle: $A = \pi r^2$

• Circumference of circle: $C = 2\pi r$

• Area of rectangle: $A = \ell w$

• Area of triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2} b h$

Volume Formulas

• Rectangular prism: $V = \ell w h$

• Right cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$

• Sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

• Right cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$

• Pyramid: $V = \frac{1}{3} B h$

Right Triangles & Angles

• Pythagorean Theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

• Special Right Triangles:

- $45^{\circ}-45^{\circ}-90^{\circ}$: $x, x, x\sqrt{2}$

- $30^{\circ}-60^{\circ}-90^{\circ}$: $x, x\sqrt{3}, 2x$

Constants & Facts

• Number of degrees of arc in a circle = 360

• Number of radians of arc in a circle = $2\pi$

• The sum of measures of degrees in a triangle = 180
